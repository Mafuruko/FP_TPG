# Development roadmap

1. **Balance a full village run.** Check food consumption, travel times, research costs, family growth and the offline cap with real players.
2. **Improve activity feedback.** Show target names, work progress and what each villager plans to do next directly on the island.
3. **Expand the build menu.** Integrate additional studio buildings when their gameplay purpose and valid placement zones are defined.
4. **Deepen exploration.** Add clues to the island itself and test the pacing of rare collectible spawns and landmark discoveries.
